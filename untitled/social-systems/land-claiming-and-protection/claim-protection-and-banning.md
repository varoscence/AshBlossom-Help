# 🛡️ Claim Protection & Banning

Take full control of your space. Use these commands to manage who is allowed to set foot on your land and ensure the safety of your borders.

***

#### 🚫 Restricted Access

These commands are used to permanently block individuals or groups from entering your claim.

| **Command**                | **Description**                         | **Aliases**       |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| `/banfromclaim [player]`   | Banish a player from your land.         | `bfc`, `banfc`    |
| `/banfromclaimall`         | Banish everyone except those you trust. | `bfca`, `bfcall`  |
| `/unbanfromclaim [player]` | Pardon a player and allow re-entry.     | `ubfc`, `unbanfc` |

Note: Banished players are physically repelled from the claim borders. They cannot enter until you manually unban them.

***

#### 👢 Quick Removal

Need someone gone immediately but don't want a permanent ban? Use the kick command.

* Command: `/kickfromclaim [player]`
* Aliases: `kfc`, `kickfc`
* Permission: `bfc.kick`
* Effect: Teleports the player outside your claim boundaries instantly.

***

#### 📋 Management & Safety

Stay organized and prevent accidental player deaths (especially in The End).

**View Ban List**

Not sure who is currently blocked?

* Command: `/banfromclaimlist`
* Aliases: `bfcl`, `bfclist`

**Set Safespot 📍**

If your claim is in the sky use this to set a location where banished players will land safely.

* Command: `/bfcsafespot`
* Aliases: `bfcs`, `bfcsetsafe`

Pro-Tip: Always set a safespot if your claim is in The End or high in the air to prevent players from falling when banished.
