# 🐬 Zoralis Market

***

Across the reefs lies Zoralis, a bustling island where traders gather to exchange rare goods and discoveries from across the realm. Over time, it has evolved into the heart of AshBlossom’s commerce—a lively market where every stall holds a treasure and every merchant adds to the server's history.

***

{% hint style="info" icon="umbrella-simple" %}
#### To Own a store you need any Rank either purchased or voting. Check the [Store to unlock them.](https://store.ashblossom.com)
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" icon="sunglasses" %}
Active Shopkeepers: All shopkeepers must be in use and not kept in chests. If you no longer need your shopkeeper, you must return it to staff for later use.
{% endhint %}

#### 📝 Applying for a Shop

Ready to start your own business? Follow these steps to secure your space:

1. Apply using this [**Form**](https://forms.gle/VwUFtm96zp3n9nBZ7)
2. In your application, indicate what you wish to sell, your build style, and if you have a preferred plot. While we cannot make promises, we will consider preferences whenever possible! Shop plots are first-come, first-serve.&#x20;
3. Once at capacity, a wait-list will be opened to ensure organized and fair plot distribution.
4. Plot Size: Every shop is a standard 11 × 11 space, perfect for a boutique or a specialized stall.

***

#### ⚖️ Shop Rules

To keep the market thriving and lag-free, all merchants must follow these guidelines:These features require a Rank. Check the [Store](https://store.ashblossom.com) to unlock them.

* Stay Active: Shops must be restocked at least once per month to remain open.
* No Auto-Farms: Automatic farms are strictly prohibited inside or beneath your shop.
* Performance: Display limits (frames/armor stands) may apply to ensure a smooth experience for all visitors.

***

#### ⏳ The Plot Waiting List

To ensure fairness and prevent abandoned storefronts, we use a structured waiting list system.

How it works:

* Join the Queue: Ranked players can sign up for the waiting list through this [**Form**](https://forms.gle/1JtMWwx1yguUkLKt9)
* The Notification: When a plot opens up, the next player in line is contacted via Discord.
* The Window: You have 8 hours to respond once notified.
* Missed Turns: If you don’t respond in time, you’ll be moved to the bottom of the list to keep things moving.&#x20;

> Why we do this: This system prevents "ghost shops" and ensures that the most active traders have a chance to grow the district!
