# 🦆 Duck Hunt

Runners race across the arena while Shooters try to knock them off with Wind Charges!

***

### 🎮 How it works

There are two teams.

**🏃 Runners** cross the arena level by level. There are 5 levels.

* When a round opens, that level's entry door slides open. Get through before it closes!
* A warning tells you when the entry closes in 10 seconds. After that, finish crossing to the exit.
* If you get knocked off, you respawn back at the arena and try again when the doors open next.

**💨 Shooters** stand in the shooter area.

* You get Wind Charges automatically during the game.
* Knock the runners off before they make it across.

### 🏆 How to win

The first runner to complete all 5 levels wins!

> [!TIP]
> The doors open in a sweep from one side, and the side changes every round. Watch which half opens first.

> [!WARNING]
> Your inventory may be cleared at the start, so don't bring anything you want to keep.
