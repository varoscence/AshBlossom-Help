# ✨ Cosmetics

AshBlossom SMP’s cosmetics system lets players customize their identities without affecting gameplay balance. Players can craft their own signature look or coordinate themes with friends, towns, or entire factions. \
\
Whether you want to trail magical effects behind you, ride across the world on unique mounts, or adventure with adorable pets at your side, customize yourself from an ever-growing library of cosmetics.
