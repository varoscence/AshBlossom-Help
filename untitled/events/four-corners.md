# 🟥 Four Corners

Pick a corner and hope it stays. The last player standing wins!

***

### 🎮 How it works

* The arena has four colored platforms.
* When the round starts, choose one platform and stand on it.
* Each round, one platform vanishes. Anyone standing on it falls and is out.
* The platforms come back for the next round, and you pick again.
* In **hard mode**, two platforms vanish each round.

### 🏆 How to win

Be the last player left on a platform.

{% hint style="success" %}
Don't follow the crowd. A busy corner is no safer than an empty one.
{% endhint %}
