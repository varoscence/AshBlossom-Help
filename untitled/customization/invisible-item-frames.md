# 🖼️ Invisible Item Frames

Enhance your builds and create seamless decorations with our **Invisible Item Frames** perk! This feature allows you to hide the background of an item frame, making items look like they are sitting naturally on blocks, shelves, or floating in mid-air.

***

#### 🪙 How to Unlock

Invisible Item Frames are a premium cosmetic perk obtainable using **Shards**.

* Type `/shop` in-game to browse the Perks category and unlock it!

***

#### 🛠️ How to Use

We have simplified the process so you no longer need long, complicated commands. You can toggle item frames between visible and invisible with a simple command and a touch.

**Step 1: Enable Toggle Mode**

To begin editing your item frames, type the following command in chat:

```bash
/frame
```

{% hint style="success" %}
_This command toggles your interaction mode on and off._
{% endhint %}

**Step 2: Transform your Frames**

1. Place an item frame and put your desired item inside it.
2. While `/frame` mode is active, simply Right-Click (touch) the item frame.
3. The frame will instantly disappear, leaving only your item visible!
4. To make it reappear: Simply right-click the item again while in `/frame` mode.

Quick Tip: Once you are done decorating, type `/frame` again to turn off the editing mode so you don't accidentally toggle your other frames while playing!&#x20;

***

#### 💡 Creative Decorating Ideas

Now that you have invisible frames, try out these cool decoration styles:

* Kitchen & Dining: Place food items (like steak, bread, or apples) directly on tables or pressure plates to look like served meals.
* Armories & Workshops: Hang weapons, tools, or shields directly onto walls without the clunky wooden background.
* Storage Rooms: Place an item representing what is inside a chest directly on the block above or in front of it for a clean, modern chest room.

Happy decorating, blossoms! 🌸
