# 📜 Server Rules & Guidelines

_Welcome to AshBlossom! To keep our "Never Reset World" enjoyable for everyone, please follow these guidelines. Common sense is our #1 rule!_

***

### 🏛️ General Conduct

❀ Be Respectful: We have zero tolerance for bullying, toxic drama, or hate speech. Be kind to your fellow players. 🌸 ❀ Keep it PG-13: No NSFW content or excessive profanity. Please avoid highly sensitive or controversial topics in public chat. ❀ Communication: Stick to English in all public channels to ensure staff can assist everyone effectively. ❀ No Spam: Avoid flooding the chat with repetitive messages, links, or unnecessary pings. ❀ Privacy First: Never share your (or others') personal real-life information. Stay safe! 🔒

***

### ⚔️ Minecraft SMP Rules

❀ No Griefing or Stealing: Damaging builds or taking items is strictly prohibited—even if the land is unclaimed. Respect the grind! ⚒️ ❀ Fair Play: No hacking, X-raying (including packs), fly-hacks, or auto-clickers. Using exploits or "loopholes" is a bannable offense. ❀ PVP Ethics: Both players must have PVP toggled ON to fight.

**To keep the server smooth and lag-free, Stasis Chambers and Chunk Loaders are disabled.**

* _Note: Killing players via lava, drowning, or traps to bypass PVP plugins is forbidden._ ❀ Server Health: Do not build massive lag-inducing redstone clocks or "lag machines." Staff may remove structures that hurt performance. ⚡

***
