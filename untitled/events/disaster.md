# 🌪️ Disaster

Survive 5 minutes of chaos! A new disaster hits every 30 seconds, and anyone still standing at the end wins.

***

### 🎮 How it works

* Players join a queue. The round starts once enough players are in.
* A new disaster arrives **every 30 seconds**. Up to 3 can run at once. When a new one arrives, the oldest one ends.
* The **sidebar** shows the time left, how many players are alive, when the next disaster hits and which disasters are active. It also lists everyone alive with their hearts, lowest first. Sneak to see more of the list.
* If you die, you watch the rest of the round as a spectator.

### 🏆 How to win

Survive until the timer runs out. If you're the **last one standing**, you win early.

### 🎁 Powerups

Look for light beams with a floating name. You can see them through walls.

* **Throwing TNT**
* **Leap Feather**
* **Fire Resistance**
* **Bandage**
* **Armor:** Hard Hat, Gold Chestplate, Gold Leggings and Feather Boots. They're put on automatically if that slot is empty.

### ☄️ Disasters you might face

| Disaster | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Meteor Shower | Fireballs rain from the sky. |
| Flood | Water rises and stings. Get high. |
| Floor is Lava | Every block you step off glows, then turns to lava behind you. Standing still too long heats your own block. |
| Sinkholes | The ground rumbles, then a hole opens and slowly grows. Stay away from the crumbling edge. |
| Tornado | A twister sucks you in and flings players and blocks. |
| Acid Rain / Solar Flare | Out in the open means damage. Find a roof. Water or Fire Resistance also stops Solar Flare. |
| TNT Rain / Anvil Rain | Look up. Red marks on the ground show where TNT will land. |
| Zombies / Withers | Mobs hunt you down. |
| Lightning | Sparks on the ground mean a strike is coming. |
| Fragile Ground | Blocks crack while you stand on them and break if you stay. |
| Blackout | You're blinded. |
| Half Health | Your max health is halved. |
| Bat Swarm | Bats chase you and knock you around. Punch one to scare it off. |
| Purge | PvP is on! |
| Swappage | Everyone swaps places. |
| Red Light | Stop the moment it turns red. There's no warning. |
| Keep Moving | Stand still and you take damage. |
| No Jumping | Nobody can jump. |
| Hot Potato | Someone holds a lit potato. Hit another player to pass it before it explodes. |
| Vampire | A player becomes a red-glowing vampire with a deadly gold sword. Disasters can't hurt them, but you can. |
| Nuke | Get off the yellow zone before it goes off, then stay out of the glowing green crater. |

> [!TIP]
> You can punch out fire on the ground. High ground beats Flood, roofs beat Acid Rain and Solar Flare, and standing still loses to Fragile Ground.
