# ⛏️ Resource Worlds

Resource Worlds are temporary dimensions designed for heavy mining, gathering, and exploration. Using these worlds helps keep our main AshBlossom landscape beautiful and "un-scarred" by massive quarries.

***

#### ⚠️ Important Information

To keep these worlds functioning as intended, please keep the following in mind:

* No Land Claims: Claiming is strictly disabled. Your builds are not protected here.
* Gathering Only: These worlds are meant for harvesting materials, not for building permanent bases.
* No Refunds: When a world resets, everything inside is deleted. Do not leave valuable items in chests or build anything you aren't prepared to lose!

***

#### 📅 Reset Schedule

We reset these dimensions regularly to ensure a fresh supply of ores, structures, and materials.

| Dimension             | Reset Frequency    |
| --------------------- | ------------------ |
| Resource Overworld 🌍 | Resets Randomly    |
| Resource Nether 🔥    | Resets Randomly    |
| The End 🌌            | Resets for events! |

***

#### 🚀 How to Access

Ready to fill your inventory? Use these commands to jump in:

* /rw — Teleport to the Resource Overworld.
* /nether — Teleport to the Resource Nether.
