# 🔍 Scavenge Hunt

Hidden items are scattered around the world. Can you find them all?

***

### 🎮 How it works

* Staff hide special items around an area.
* When you find one, click it. A message tells you how many you've found and how many are left.
* Each item only counts once for you, so keep exploring to find the rest.
* Several hunts can run at the same time, each with its own items.

### 🏆 How to win

Find every hidden item in the hunt. When you find the last one, you get a celebration, plus a reward when the hunt has one. If you're the **first to finish**, you're told, and so are the staff.

{% hint style="success" %}
Look up, look down and look behind things. The best hiding spots are the ones you walk past every day.
{% endhint %}
