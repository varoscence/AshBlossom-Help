# 🆘 Support & Contact

Need a hand? Whether you’ve encountered a bug, need to report a player, or have a question about your rank, our team is here to help!

***

#### 🎟️ Open a Support Ticket

For the fastest response, please use our Ticket System on Discord. This creates a private conversation between you and the AshBlossom staff.

* [Open a Ticket Here](https://discord.com/channels/1466579163623194686/1468294775923937390) 🎫

Please use tickets for:

* Purchase or Store issues.
* Reporting griefing or rule-breakers.
* Technical support and connection issues.
* Requesting an official Partner ceremony.

***

#### 💬 Join Our Community

Most general questions can be answered by our amazing community members in our public channels!

* [Official Discord Server](https://discord.gg/aZh4fhAShM) 🌿

***

#### 💡 Before Opening a Ticket...

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

1. Your In-Game Name (IGN) — specify if you are a Bedrock player (with the `*`).
2. The World/Dimension — are you in the Overworld, Zoralis, or the Art World?
3. Screenshots or Coordinates — visual evidence helps us track down problems much faster!
