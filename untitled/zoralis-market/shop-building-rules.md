# 🏛️ Shop Building Rules

Violations may result in warnings, forced edits, or shop removal. \
Unnecessarily high builds are not allowed.

***

### **📦 Block Entities & Limits (Per Shop)**

* Armor Stands: Max 1
* Chests: Max 2 per shopkeeper
* Ender Chest: Max 1 per store
* Campfires: Max 3
* Banners Max 3
* Shelves: Allowed freely (Excessive overuse will be flagged)\
  **- No slot Machines -**

### **🖼️ Decorative Entities (Per Shop)**

* Item Frames: Max 4 (Normal & Glow combined)
* Paintings: Max 4
* Player Heads: Max 3
* Signs: Max 5 (All types combined)

### **🚫 Fully Banned (No Exceptions)**

* Beacons | Redstone Clocks (Any form) | Spawner Blocks

### **⚙️ Redstone & Automation**

* Hoppers: Max 5 per shop.
* Pistons: Decorative use only; no automated systems.
* The use of automatic jukeboxes is prohibited. Standard jukeboxes are permitted.
* Comparators: May not feed into active redstone chains/contraptions.
* Automation: No automated redstone machines of any kind inside shops.
