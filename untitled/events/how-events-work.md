# 📅 How Events Work

Most events and minigames on AshBlossom are hosted by staff. Here's how to catch them and what to expect.

***

### 📣 Finding out about events

* **Discord:** events are announced in the news channel with the date and time. Hit **Interested** on the Discord Event to get a ping before it starts.
* **In game:** staff post reminders in chat during the day, and again 30 and 10 minutes before the start.
* Some minigames are spontaneous. If the server is lively, staff may start one with little notice, so keep an eye on chat.

### 🌀 Getting there

About 10 minutes before an event starts, staff open the way in. Jump through the **Events Portal** at Spawn to reach the event area. The portal closes when the event is over.

### 🎮 During the event

* The host explains the rules and announces the prizes before the first round.
* Most games start with a big **3, 2, 1, GO!** countdown on your screen.
* Follow the host's lead. They have the final say on every call.
* Some events have a Discord voice channel named after the event. Joining is optional.

### 🏆 Prizes

Winners are announced in chat. Many events also give a reward just for taking part.

> [!TIP]
> Empty your inventory before you join. Some games clear or replace your inventory when they start.

### 📸 After the event

Screenshots and clips go in the event's forum on Discord. Questions about an event can be asked in the Discord help channel.
