# 🤝 Socials: Friends, Besties & Partners 💍

> ⚠️ Note: These features require a Rank. Check the [Store](https://store.ashblossom.com) to unlock them.

***

Friends, besties and partners, all in one place. Type `/socials` to open the menu, or `/socials help` for the full command list.

You can have **one partner**, **one bestie** and as many **friends** as you like.

***

#### 👋 Friends

Add friends, sort them into groups and teleport to them.

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/socials friend add <player>` | Send a friend request. |
| `/socials friend accept <player>` | Accept a friend request. |
| `/socials friend decline <player>` | Decline a friend request. |
| `/socials friend remove <player>` | Remove a friend. |
| `/socials friend list` | See your friends and who's online. |
| `/socials friend nick <player> <nickname>` | Give a friend a nickname only you can see. |
| `/socials friend note <player> [note]` | Save a private note about a friend. |
| `/socials friend sharepositions <player>` | Send your coordinates to a friend. |
| `/socials ftp <player>` | Teleport to a friend. |
| `/socials ftp toggle` | Stop friends from teleporting to you. |
| `/socials friend notifications toggle` | Turn friend join and leave messages on or off. |

**Groups:**

* `/socials friend group create <name>` — Create a group, like "Builders" or "Redstoners".
* `/socials friend group set <player> <group>` — Put a friend in a group.
* `/socials friend groupchat <group> on` — Chat only with one group. Use `off` to stop.

***

#### 💛 Besties

Your bestie is your one closest friend.

**How to become besties:**

1. Type `/socials bestie <friend>`.
2. Your friend types `/socials bestie accept`, or clicks **Accept**.
3. You both get a celebration, and everyone online hears about it!

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/socials bestie tp` | Teleport to your bestie, even if they turned friend teleports off. |
| `/socials bestie gift` | Give your bestie the item in your hand. |
| `/socials bestie handshake` | A secret handshake. Stand next to each other. |
| `/socials bestie highfive` | A high five. Stand next to each other. |
| `/socials bestie shareclaims on` | Let your bestie build in all your claims. You both have to turn it on. |
| `/socials bestie end` | Stop being besties. Either of you can do this. |

**Bestie perk:** standing near your bestie gives you both bonus XP.

***

#### 💍 Partners

A partnership is the closest bond on AshBlossom.

**How to become partners:**

1. Hold your own ceremony, anywhere you like. We highly recommend a celebration at your base or in Zoralis!
2. Ask a **Magistrate** to make it official. See who's online with `/socials partner magistrates`.
3. You both accept, and everyone online hears about it!

| Command | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `/socials partner tp` | Teleport to your partner. |
| `/socials partner home` | Teleport to your shared home. |
| `/socials partner sethome` | Set your shared home. |
| `/socials partner delhome` | Delete your shared home. |
| `/socials partner gift` | Give your partner the item in your hand. |
| `/socials partner hug` | Hug your partner. You can also sneak and right-click them. |
| `/socials partner megasecrethandshake` | Only partners know it. |
| `/socials partner color` | Pick the color of your heart in the tab list. |
| `/socials partner seen` | See when your partner was last online. |
| `/socials partner list` | See everyone who has a partner. |

**Partner perks:** standing near your partner gives you both bonus XP and faster healing.

***

#### 🪪 Profiles

`/socials profile <player>` shows someone's partner and bestie, and since when.

{% hint style="info" %}
Teleports, gifts and the bonus XP don't work in the events world.
{% endhint %}
