# 🎨 The Map Art Gallery World

Preserving our landscape while empowering our creators.

To keep our "Never Reset world" pristine and natural, all large-scale map art has moved to a dedicated Map Art Dimension. This specialized world offers a flat, grief-protected environment optimized for massive creative projects.

***

#### 🚀 How to Become an Artist

Access to the Art World is Open!

* Request Staff to give you a plot!

#### 🛠️ The Plot System

Every artist is assigned a pre-defined, admin-protected workspace.

* Foundation: Plots are set on a flat obsidian/bedrock floor—no more clearing land.
* Security: Your work is 100% safe from griefing and overlapping builds.
* Collaboration: Plot owners can add up to two helpers to assist with construction.

#### 👁️ Visiting the Gallery

You don't need to be an artist to appreciate the work!

* Touring: Any player with the perk can teleport to the Art World to watch artists in action.
* Squaremap: View the entire gallery from your browser via our [\[Web Map Link\]](http://map.ashblossom.com:8080/#minecraft_mapart;flat;1000,64,476;3).
* Restrictions: Visitors can explore freely but cannot break or place blocks.

#### ⚖️ Why the Change?

| Survival World                                                          | Map Art World                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Natural & Wild: No more massive obsidian grids or "scarred" landscapes. | Optimized for Art: Flat, clean, and perfectly sized for maps. + Obsidian base |
| Performance: Reduced lag by moving entity-heavy art elsewhere.          | Showcased: Your work is featured in a dedicated digital gallery.              |
| Organic Growth: Pure survival builds only.                              | Creative Freedom: Build as big as you want without ruining the view.          |
