# ❄️ Spleef

Dig the floor out from under everyone else. The last player standing wins!

***

### 🎮 How it works

* Everyone gathers on the glass lobby floor above the arena. Only players standing on it when the game starts take part.
* At the start, the lobby floor drops and you fall onto the arena: several platforms stacked on top of each other.
* Everyone gets a fast **Diamond Shovel** to break the floor and a **Second Chance** feather.
* Break the blocks under other players so they fall to the platform below. Fall below the last platform and you're out.

### 🪶 Second Chance

Right-click the feather to rise **one platform** back up. It works once per game, and not from the top platform.

### ⏳ No hiding

If you're left alone on a platform far behind everyone else, you get a 5-second warning to catch up. If nobody joins you before it ends, your platform collapses.

### 🏆 How to win

Be the last player still on a platform.

> [!TIP]
> Keep moving. Standing still makes you an easy target, and the floor can disappear under you at any moment.
