# 👥 Staff Team

{% hint style="info" %}
**This page fills the cards on staff.ashblossom.com.** Changes show on the site within about 2 minutes.

* **## Founders / Moderators / Helpers** are the groups. **### Name** is one card, in the order shown.
* Under each name: **Minecraft name** (for the 3D figure), **Animal** (fox, turtle, bunny, cat, bee, mushroom, blossom, sunflower, bigflower or ursulita), optionally **Skin id** and **Button**.
* Every paragraph after that is the bio. Use plain text without quote marks, and the curly apostrophe ’.
{% endhint %}
