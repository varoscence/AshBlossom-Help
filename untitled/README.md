# 🌸 Welcome to AshBlossom

***

#### 📖 The Lore Fragment: _The Birth of AshBlossom_

> _A celestial phoenix once fell from the heavens, its heart shattering and its eternal flames scattering across a cold, lifeless void. Where its sacred ash touched the earth, the world awakened in a burst of color and growth. That world was named AshBlossom—a land born of fire, destined to bloom forever._

***

#### 🌳 The "Never Reset" Promise

AshBlossom SMP is a refined, mostly-vanilla survival experience built on one unbreakable foundation: The world never resets.

* Your Legacy is Permanent: Every castle, hidden base, and sprawling road remains part of our history forever.
* Expansion, Not Erasure: We don’t wipe progress. When Minecraft updates, we simply expand our borders, revealing fresh terrain and new resources while keeping your old home untouched.
* A Living History: Join a world where you can walk through years of player-built history and add your own chapter to the legend.
