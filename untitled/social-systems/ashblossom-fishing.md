# 🎣 AshBlossom Fishing

Fishing on AshBlossom is more than cod and salmon. Every fish you reel in has a **rarity** and a **length**, and the longest catches each month win prizes!

***

### How it works

Just fish like normal with any fishing rod. Every catch has:

* **A rarity:** Junk, Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary. The rarer it is, the harder it is to get.
* **A length** in centimeters. Every catch is different, even two of the same fish.

Every fish you catch is saved in your **Fish Journal**, forever.

***

### Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/fish` | Opens the fishing menu: the bait shop, your journal and help |
| `/fish applybaits` | Opens a menu to put baits on the rod in your hand |
| `/fish journal` | Shows every fish you've caught |
| `/fish toggle` | Turns custom fishing on or off for you |
| `/fish top` | Shows this month's longest fish in each rarity |
| `/fish help` | Lists all of these commands in-game |

***

### 🍳 Eating & Cooking

Fish that are real fish items (**cod, salmon, tropical fish and pufferfish**) can be **eaten** or **cooked** in a furnace, just like normal.

* Fish shown as heads or other items (like the Starfish or Spongebob) can't be eaten. They're trophies.
* Careful with **pufferfish**, they're still poisonous!
* Eating or cooking a fish **doesn't** take it off the leaderboard. Your catch already counted the moment you reeled it in.

> ⚠️ **Don't drink the Epic Elixir!** It's a bait, not a drink. Drinking it uses it up.

***

### 🪱 Bait Shop

Baits make certain fish more likely to bite. You buy them with **diamonds**, which is the only currency the bait shop takes.

**To buy:** run `/fish`, click **View Baits**, then click a bait **twice** to confirm. The diamonds come straight out of your inventory.

| Bait           | Price  | What it does                                                                |
| -------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shrimps        | 3 💎   | 2x more Carp, Bluefish, Haddock (Common) and Sunfish, Goldfish, Nemo (Rare) |
| Stringy Worms  | 8 💎   | 2x more Common and Legendary fish                                           |
| Epic Elixir    | 12 💎  | 2x more Epic fish                                                           |
| Legendary Lure | 16 💎  | 2x more Legendary fish                                                      |
| Fresh Water    | 24 💎  | 2x more Epic and Legendary fish                                             |
| Ender Lure     | 128 💎 | **4x** more Legendary fish. The strongest Legendary bait                    |

* Each purchase gives you 1 bait.
* Only plain diamonds work. Renamed or special diamonds are never taken.
* Every bait is **used up**, one per fish you catch.
* You can also find baits while fishing!

***

### 🎣 Putting bait on your rod

You must be in **Survival** mode. There are two ways:

1. **Drag and drop:** open your inventory (**E**), pick up the bait, and click it **onto your fishing rod**. Hold **Shift** while doing it to apply the whole stack.
2. **Menu:** hold your fishing rod and run `/fish applybaits`. Put your baits in the menu, then close it.

The baits on your rod are listed in its tooltip.

***

### 🏆 Monthly Longest Fish Leaderboard

Every month there's a race for the **longest Legendary, Epic and Rare fish**.

* Your **longest** fish of each rarity is your entry. Catch a longer one and it replaces it.
* You can only hold one spot per rarity, but you can be on all three boards.
* If two fish are the exact same length, whoever caught theirs first keeps the spot.
* Check the standings with `/fish top` or on the leaderboard hologram at spawn.

**At the end of each month, the #1 in each rarity wins:**

| Rarity       | Prize         |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| 🟨 Legendary | Ashwarden Key |
| 🟪 Epic      | Cosmetics Key |
| 🟦 Rare      | Tools Key     |

Prizes are given automatically, even if you're offline. The board resets on the **1st of every month** and everyone starts fresh. Your Fish Journal is never reset.

***

### ♻️ Composting, Lava & Cactus

Fish behave like normal items:

* **Composting:** right-click a composter with a fish to compost it. Just like other items, not every fish adds a layer.
* **Lava, fire and cactus** destroy fish and baits, so keep your trophies away from them!

***

### 🍄 The Nate-Fish

A very special Legendary fish, made in honor of Nate, the first player to collect every fish. Good luck catching it!

### ❓ FAQ

**Bait won't go on my rod?** Make sure you're in Survival mode. Each bait also has a limit per rod.

**"That bait is not for sale"?** That bait can't be bought right now. Let staff know.

**"You cannot afford that bait"?** You need the full price in plain diamonds in your inventory.

**Can I sell my fish?** No. Fish selling is turned off on AshBlossom.

**If I eat my record fish, do I lose my spot?** No. Your spot is saved the moment you catch it.
