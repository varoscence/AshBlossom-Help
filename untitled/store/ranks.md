# 👑 Ranks

Ranks are optional upgrades that support AshBlossom SMP and unlock extra commands, more homes, and one-time rewards. Ranks are purchased from the official store: [**store.ashblossom.com**](https://store.ashblossom.com/)

### 🌱 How Ranks Work

* **Every rank includes everything from the ranks below it.** For example, Starseeker gets all Emberling and Flamebound perks, plus its own.
* **Commands** are unlocked as soon as you have the rank.
* **Homes** and **Player Warps** are limits. Higher ranks raise your limit.
* **Rewards** (Shards, Bonus Claim Blocks, Shop Slots and Shopkeepers) are given with the rank.
* **Ranks can be gifted.** Use the 🎁 button next to "Add to Basket" on the store to buy a rank for another player.
