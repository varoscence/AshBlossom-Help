# 🏁 AshKart Races

A kart-style boat race on ice! Drift, slide and outsmart the other racers.

***

### 🎮 How it works

* Each racer gets a boat on the starting grid.
* After a **3, 2, 1** countdown, the boats are released.
* Race through every checkpoint, lap after lap. The host sets how many laps.
* Drive through **item boxes** to pick up items and use them against other racers. You can hold two at a time.
* **Boost pads** speed you up and **jump pads** launch you into the air.

### 🎁 Items you can find

Boost, Ink, Shell, Trap, Barrier, Boombox, Reverse, Poison, Fireball and Snipe. Each item box gives you one, and a box needs a few seconds before it can be used again.

### 🏆 How to win

Be the first racer across the finish line after the last lap.

### ⏱️ Time trials

Between events you can race alone against the clock to set your best lap. The fastest times show on the AshKart leaderboard at Spawn.
