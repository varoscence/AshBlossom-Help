# 🗳️ Voting

***

Support the server and earn Vote Keys 🗝️ to unlock crates filled with amazing rewards! 🎁\
\
⚠️**Keys are Virtual not physical**🗝️

#### ⚠️ Important: Check Your.Name!

Before you click any links, ensure you are using the correct format for your username:

* Java Players 💻: Enter your exact Minecraft username (e.g., `MinecraftPlayer`).
* Bedrock Players 📱: You MUST add a `.` (period) before your name (e.g., `.MinecraftPlayer`).

> 💡 Note: If you forget the dot, the server won't know who to give the key to!

#### 💎 How to Claim Your Rewards

1. Stay Online 🎮: Try to be logged into the server while you vote to ensure the key hits your inventory instantly.
2. Check Your Inventory 🎒: You will receive a Vote Key 🗝️ for every site you vote on.
3. Head to Spawn 📍: Type `/spawn` in the game chat.
4. Find the Crates 📦: Look for the Vote Crate at the spawn area.
5. Right-Click to Win ✨: Hold your Vote Key and right-click the crate to see what reward you won!

***

#### 🔥 Pro Tips

* Daily Rewards 📅: You can vote on all four links every 24 hours.
* Inventory Space 🧹: Make sure you have an empty slot in your inventory before you right-click the crate!
* Vote Parties 🎉: Every vote brings the server closer to a Vote Party, where _everyone_ online gets a bonus reward.
