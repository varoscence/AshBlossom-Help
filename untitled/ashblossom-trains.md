# 🚂 AshBlossom Trains

Build your own working train! Link a locomotive and up to 16 cars together, and they move as one. Use it to get around the world, run a passenger line between bases, or build a rollercoaster.

***

### 🛠️ Crafting a Train

Put these in a crafting table (any layout works):

| Ingredient            | Amount |
| --------------------- | ------ |
| Minecart with Furnace | 1      |
| Minecart              | 1 to 8 |

You get a **Train** item. Each minecart you add is one car, so a crafting table makes up to **8 cars**.

> 💡 The recipes are in your recipe book. Search "train".

***

### 🛤️ Placing Your Train

1. Build some track. Curves and slopes are fine.
2. Hold your Train and **right-click a rail**.
3. The locomotive appears where you clicked, and the cars line up behind it toward you.

You need enough rail in a row for the whole train. If it doesn't fit, you keep your Train and get a message saying how many rails you need.

> 💡 Rule of thumb: about 1.5 rails per car, plus one for the locomotive.

***

### 🚃 Adding More Cars

Want a longer train? Hold a **Minecart** and **right-click any car** of your placed train. A new car is coupled onto the back.

* Trains can have up to **16 cars**.
* There has to be free track behind the last car.
* Each new car uses up one Minecart.

***

### 🎮 Driving

1. Sit in the **front car**, the one right behind the locomotive.
2. **Right-click the locomotive** to open **✿ AshBlossom Trains**.
3. Add fuel, pick a speed, and off you go!

Only the player in the front car can open the controls. Nobody can sit in the locomotive itself.

#### The controls

| Button                             | What it does                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stop**                           | Brakes the train to a stop                              |
| **Slow / Normal / Fast / Express** | Picks your speed                                        |
| **Forward / Reverse**              | Changes direction (the train stops first)               |
| **Auto**                           | Lets the train run by itself (see below)                |
| **Smoke**                          | Left-click: Low / Medium / High. Right-click: on or off |
| **Add fuel**                       | Click fuel in your own inventory to load it             |

If you get out of the front car while driving, the train brakes to a stop (unless it's on Auto).

***

### ⚡ Speeds & Fuel

Better fuel unlocks faster speeds:

| Speed   | Blocks per second | Fuel needed      |
| ------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| Slow    | 4                 | Coal or Charcoal |
| Normal  | 8                 | Coal or Charcoal |
| Fast    | 10                | Coal Block       |
| Express | 12                | Blaze Rod        |

Faster speeds burn fuel quicker:

| Fuel              | Lasts about                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------ |
| 1 Coal / Charcoal | 6 min on Slow, 3 min on Normal |
| 1 Coal Block      | 13.5 min on Fast               |
| 1 Blaze Rod       | 2 min 40 s on Express          |

* Clicking fuel in your inventory loads the **whole stack** at once.
* Fuel can't be taken back out, but it stays with your train. When you pick the train up, the leftover fuel goes into the Train item.
* When the fuel for your speed runs out, the train drops to the fastest speed your remaining fuel allows.

***

### 🤖 Auto Mode

Auto lets your train run with **nobody in it**, which is perfect for passenger lines and rollercoasters.

* **Loops** run nonstop.
* **Lines with dead ends** go back and forth: the train stops at each end, waits 10 seconds, then heads back.
* **Auto never uses fuel**, and every speed works, including Express.

**Two ways to turn it on:**

* From the front car, open the controls and click **Auto**.
* Or, without getting in: **right-click the locomotive while holding coal, charcoal, a coal block or a blaze rod**. One item goes into the tank, Auto turns on, and a stopped train starts at Normal.

To change the speed or turn Auto off, sit in the front car and open the controls.

> 💡 Trains pause when no players are nearby and carry on when someone comes back.

***

### 🛡️ Built-in Safety

Your train looks after itself:

* **Slows down before curves and slopes** so it stays on the track.
* **Stops by itself before the end of the line** instead of flying off.
* **Keeps its cars together**, even with two tracks side by side.
* **Goes straight through mobs** instead of scooping them up, so a cow on the line won't stop you.

***

### 📦 Picking Up Your Train

**Break any cart** and the whole train turns back into **one Train item**, with every car and its leftover fuel. A 14-car train places again as 14 cars.

***

### ❓ Good to Know

* Trains can't go through Nether or End portals.
* Two trains on the same track don't see each other. Give each train its own line, or they'll bump into each other.
* You can't place a train in someone else's claim.
* Just like normal minecarts, other players can break a train that isn't in a claim. Keep yours somewhere safe.
* Riding at Express is fast! On curves and hills the train slows down on purpose.
