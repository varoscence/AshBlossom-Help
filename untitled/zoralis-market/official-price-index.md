# 🏛️ Official Price Index

To keep AshBlossom SMP a rewarding world with balance, the Zoralis Market uses a strictly managed diamond economy. These price ranges are mandatory for all shops.

### ⚖️ The Minimum Price Rule

You cannot sell items below the listed minimum price, even for partial stacks. For example, if the minimum is 2 Diamonds per stack, a half-stack (32 items) still costs 2 Diamonds. This protects the server in three ways:

* **No "Fractional" Pricing:** We do not allow "shaving" prices for smaller amounts. This prevents a loophole where players try to trade for "half-diamonds" or junk items to bypass the minimum fee.
* **Value Protection:** If prices drop too low, diamonds lose their buying power. A stable "price floor" ensures the diamonds you mine stay valuable long-term.
* **Fair Competition:** Minimums prevent a "race to the bottom" where shops undercut each other until trading becomes pointless. This ensures your hard work — whether mining or farming — is always fairly rewarded.

### 📊 Economic Balance

These ranges prevent hyper-inflation and keep the economy moving. Following these prices keeps items rare, goals meaningful, and ensures every diamond in your vault has real power. ✨

> Diamonds are the ONLY accepted currency.

***

* One shop type per shopkeeper. No mixing shops in a single shopkeeper.
* All shopkeepers must be in use and not kept in chests. If you no longer need your shopkeeper, return it to staff for later use.
* Prices are min–max. Example: "2–4 Diamonds" means 2 Diamonds minimum, 4 Diamonds maximum.
* Items marked (Any Color) share one price across all colours.
* Updated for Minecraft 26.3.

### 🚫 Important Rules

* Prices must stay within the listed ranges at all times.
* Each shopkeeper must operate within ONE category only.
* No fake rarity or renaming of items to inflate prices.
* No bundle tricks or workarounds to bypass pricing rules.
* Shopkeepers must be in use and not kept in chests; if you don't need your shopkeeper, return it to staff.
* Diamonds and Diamond Blocks may not be sold — they are the currency. Diamond Ore may be sold as a decorative block.
* Copper Lanterns and Copper Torches are Copper Shop items, not Light Shop.
* Decorative Shelves (all types) are Wood Shop items, not Book Shop.
* Colour variants share one price. Selling a Cyan Bed above a White Bed is fake rarity.
* No real estate. Claims, land, plots, bases, coordinates and pre-built structures may not be sold, traded, rented or auctioned. Labour may be sold; property may not.

⚠️ Breaking any of the above rules will result in immediate shop removal.
