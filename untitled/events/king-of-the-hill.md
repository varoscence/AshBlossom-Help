# 👑 King of the Hill

Claim the hill and knock everyone else off it!

***

### 🎮 How it works

* Everyone starts together on a spawn platform.
* After a **3, 2, 1** countdown, you drop into the fight zone.
* Use the **King's Stick** to knock other players off.
* Fall out of the fight zone and you're out.

### 🏆 How to win

Be the last player remaining on the hill.

> [!TIP]
> The edges are dangerous. Fight from the middle and let others come to you.
