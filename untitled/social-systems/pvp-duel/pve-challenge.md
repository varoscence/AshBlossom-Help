# ⚔️ PVE Challenge ☠

### ☠️ The Trials

**What is the PvE Challenge?**

The PvE Challenge is AshBlossom SMP's wave-based PVE gauntlet, available in **Solo**, **DUO**, and **GROUP** modes. Enter a private arena and face 15 escalating waves of named, geared, cursed elite mobs, ending in a boss fight against Vaelthorn, the Bound Sovereign. Each wave is harder than the last. Your completion time is recorded on a monthly leaderboard. The faster you finish, the higher you rank.

***

**How to Start**

**Solo Mode**

```
/pve solo
```

**DUO Mode** (invite a partner)

```
/pve duo <playername>
```

Your partner receives an invite and must type `/pve accept` to confirm.

**GROUP Mode** (invite four partners)

```
/pve group <player1> <player2> <player3> <player4>
```

Every invited player must type `/pve accept` — the run only begins once all four have confirmed.

If all arenas are occupied you will be placed in a queue automatically. When a slot opens you will be teleported in — no need to do anything.

> Duo and Group don't begin the moment one person walks in. Every party member has to physically cross the starting portal before wave 1 launches.

***

**The Rules**

* You have **3 lives (Solo) / 4 lives (Duo) / 6 lives (Group)**. Dying costs one life — you respawn at the start of your current wave with a fresh kit.
* Lose all lives and the run ends immediately.
* You cannot break or place blocks inside the arena.
* You cannot drop or pick up items, or open a chest, barrel, shulker box, furnace, hopper, ender chest, Decorated Pot, Item Frame, or Armor Stand, or trade with a villager. The arena is a sealed instance from start to finish.
* You cannot teleport away or use any command other than `/pve` while inside a run. **`/pve stop` is the only way out.**
* Mobs drop nothing — no loot, no XP.
* **Your mobs are yours.** Other players cannot damage mobs that aren't part of their own run, and yours can't be touched by anyone outside your party.
* Mobs target whoever's actually fighting them, not just whoever they first noticed — there's no free-hitting one from safety while it's busy with a teammate.
* If you disconnect during a Solo run, your run ends and cleans up immediately.

**DUO/GROUP-specific rules**

* Lives are **shared across the whole party**.
* If a player disconnects mid-run, they have 60 seconds to reconnect before being counted as permanently gone — the run continues with whoever remains.
* A reconnecting player is teleported back in automatically, fully re-geared.
* Group ends if the roster drops below 4 remaining players. Duo ends the moment either player is gone for good.

There are **15 waves** before the finale. Each wave introduces tougher gear and new enemy types.

> All mobs have permanent Fire Resistance — fire and lava will not save you. All mobs glow, so you can track them through walls and corridors.

**The Finale**

**Vaelthorn, the Bound Sovereign** — a single, no-minions boss fight matched in strength to a real end-game encounter. It is everything the gloom hoped you would become. It does not know you at all.

Beat it, and your time is recorded on the leaderboard.

***

**Enemy Roster**

**Faint Echo / Drifting Shade** — Armored zombies and husks. Entry-level threats, still dangerous stacked together in later waves.

**Flicker** — A small, fast baby zombie. Low threat alone, but its speed makes it a nuisance in a crowd.

**Hollow Whisper / Frozen Regret** — Bow-armed skeletons and strays, picking at you from range.

**Withering Doubt / Gloom Reaper / Veiled Reaper / Reaper of Embers** — Wither Skeletons with swords. Every hit applies Wither — watch your health bar for the black tint.

**Bitter Memory / Whispering Malice** — Witches. They spam Poison, Harming, Slowness, and Weakness. Kill them first.

**Restless Guilt** — A Pillager with a crossbow. Relentless ranged pressure.

**Ashen Warden / Ember-Scarred Warden / Warden of Ash / Warden of the Veil** — Vindicators with enchanted axes. They close distance fast and hit hard through poor shield timing.

**Hollow Ambition / Gilded Fury** — Zombified Piglins. Permanently hostile, and they never stop chasing once aggroed.

**Gloom-Touched Brute / Feral Shadow / Rampaging Shadow** — Zoglins. Pure melee, boosted by the same Strength effect as everything around them.

**Iron-Bound Regret / Unyielding Regret** — Piglin Brutes. Full diamond gear, an axe, and zero hesitation.

**THE UNBOWED REGRET / THE WARDEN ETERNAL / THE FINAL REAPER / THE LAST BITTER MEMORY / THE SHADOW UNCHAINED** — Wave 15's named elites. Every enemy family the challenge has thrown at you, at once, at maximum HP.

***

**Tips & Strategy**

**Witches are the highest priority target every wave.** Poison and Weakness stacked on top of melee pressure kills fast.

**Wither is invisible death.** When your health bar turns black, drink a Healing potion immediately. Do not ignore it.

**Every mob glows — use it.** Track enemies through walls and around corners instead of walking into a pile blind.

**Aggro follows whoever's hitting it.** There's no free-hitting a mob from safety while it's busy chasing a teammate — landing a hit pulls its attention onto you instantly.

**Arrows are limited — no Infinity.** 64 per life. Save them for ranged targets you can't easily close on.

**Ender Pearls are your escape tool.** Surrounded and need a second to breathe? Pearl to the other side.

**Wave 14 onward, every hit can inflict Wither.** Keep a Healing potion in reach at all times.

**DUO/GROUP tip — split priorities.** One player on melee, one on Witches and ranged threats. Communication matters more than raw skill.

***

**Leaderboard**

Only successful full-run completions (all 15 waves + the finale) are recorded. Your best time is saved per mode.

The leaderboard resets on the **1st of every month**, so there's always a fresh race for the fastest time. Each month's **Top 3 in Solo, Duo and Group** get an automatic reward.

```
/pve leaderboard solo
/pve leaderboard duo
/pve leaderboard group
```

***

**Checking Your Progress**

Type `/pve status` at any point during a run to see your current wave, lives remaining, mobs left, kills, and time elapsed. A title also appears on-screen at the start of every wave.
