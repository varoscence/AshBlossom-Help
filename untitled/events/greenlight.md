# 🚦 Greenlight

Cross the finish line without getting caught moving!

***

### 🎮 How it works

* When a game opens, a message appears in chat. Click it to join. You can only join once per game.
* You wait in the lobby, then you're taken to the start line behind an invisible wall that drops when the race begins.
* **GO:** the lights are up, so walk toward the finish line.
* **STOP:** everything goes dark and you hear a heartbeat. Freeze! You can look around, but don't walk or jump.
* Each GO and STOP lasts between 2 and 5 seconds, so you never know when it will change. You get half a second to stop.
* Move on STOP and the **Warden** shoots you. You're out of that game, but you keep all your items.

The counter on the right of your screen shows how many players are left, how many crossed, the time left and whether it's GO or STOP.

### ⚔️ PvP rounds

Some rounds have PvP on. You're told when you join. Other racers can hit you once the race starts, and getting pushed on STOP still gets you shot. A hit that would kill you only knocks you out of the game: you don't die or lose items.

### 🏆 How to win

The first player across the finish line wins. Everyone after them still gets a place, and anyone who crosses gets a prize.

### 💡 Good to know

* You can only walk during a race: no running or jumping. Your hunger bar looks low, but it goes back to normal afterwards.
* Thunder rolls now and then. It's only a sound and can't hurt you.
* You can't use items, fly, or use most commands during a game.
* Leaving or logging off takes you out, and you can't rejoin that game.
